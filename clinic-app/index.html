Section 3 · most important

Official certification authority

These two are the make-or-break for actually shipping a pet. A government export needs an Official Veterinarian to sign the health certificate, and a MOCCAE export endorsement. We need the honest situation on both.

For a UK (and most) export, the health certificate must be signed by a government-authorised Official Vet. This is the single most important thing to confirm.
The MOCCAE export permit + endorsement is the other government step. Often the slowest part.
